How the lock works

Each card's link opens the score entry app with ?team= set. The first time it loads on a phone, that team gets saved and locked to that device — the app hides its "Change team" option from then on, so a team can't accidentally (or deliberately) start entering scores for someone else.

Add or rename a team in the sheet, refresh this page, and hand out the new card. Nothing here is hardcoded.
