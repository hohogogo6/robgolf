04 · The Rules

How we play

2-person scramble

Captain's choice. Both partners hit, you pick the better ball, and you both play from there. Repeat until it's in the hole.

Bring a partner

Preferably have your own partner — but if you're a single, we can likely find another single for you from the group. Don't let that stop you from signing up.

Skins

Lowest score on a hole wins the skin — no ties. Whatever's left of the prize pool after payouts and raffles goes here.

Full rules & prize breakdown

→

Format. 2-Man Captain's Choice: both players hit a tee shot, pick the best one, both hit again from there, and repeat until you hole out.

→

OB / lost ball. Play all the rules of golf, except we don't penalize distance for shots out of bounds or lost. Do your best to determine where the ball went in and drop there, taking a one-stroke penalty. If it's in question, consult your foursome to agree on the best spot.

→

Pace of play. The drop rule above is meant to keep things moving — along the same lines, please cap your score at double bogey. Want to torture yourself and play it out instead? Fine, as long as you keep pace.

→

Lift, clean and place. Allowed — but don't move your ball from rough to fairway, trap to fairway, or fringe to green.

→

1st & 2nd place. Aggregate stroke play, with separate low-net (handicapped) and low-gross (no handicap) winners for each — a single team can only win net or gross, not both. A tie for a place is broken on the hardest handicap hole first, continuing hole by hole until a winner emerges; no handicap is used in the tiebreaker. If teams match on every hole, that place is split between them.

→

Long drive & closest to the pin. To win, your ball must be on the fairway (long drive) or on the green (closest to the pin).

→

Skins. Lowest score on a hole wins it — no ties. Skins aren't handicapped, and their value depends on how many are won. If no skins are won outright, we'll raffle 6 additional "skins" to players who haven't yet won a raffle or other prize.

Host's final note

This is meant to be a fun way to get together, meet new people or enjoy the company of old friends, and enjoy a game of golf. I'm doing my best to keep competition fair. As a reminder, prize pools will be adjusted based on the final number of teams and paid entries.
