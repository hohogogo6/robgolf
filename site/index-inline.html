04 · The Rules

How we play

2-person scramble

Captain's choice. Both partners hit, you pick the better ball, and you both play from there. Repeat until it's in the hole.

Bring a partner

Preferably have your own partner — but if you're a single, we can likely find another single for you from the group. Don't let that stop you from signing up.

Handicaps

We don't typically handicap, but have for a couple of teams. We'll see what the field looks like and make a decision then. The aim is to keep it fair and fun.

Skins

Lowest score on a hole wins the skin — no ties. Whatever's left of the prize pool after payouts and raffles goes here.

09 · Questions

FAQ

I don't have a partner. Can I still play?

Yes. Bring your own if you can, but we can likely find another single for you from the group. Just say so when you sign up.

Do I need to be good at golf?

No. It's a scramble, so your partner covers your bad shots and you cover theirs. A good chunk of the prize pool goes to raffles and closest-to-the-pin — things anyone can win.

Can my spouse or partner come to the party?

Absolutely. Significant others are welcome. Food is $18 per person for guests.

What time should I get there?

Tee times run consecutively from 11:33 AM. Give yourself enough time to check in and hit range balls — they're included.

Will there be handicaps?

We don't typically handicap, but have for a couple of teams. We'll look at the field and decide then. The aim is fair and fun.

How do skins work?

Lowest score on a hole wins that hole's skin, no ties. Whatever's left of the prize pool after the placings, raffles, long drive and closest-to-pins funds it.

What if it rains?

Text Rob. We'll follow the course's call and let everyone know as early as we can.
